## About Us

Elfvain Studios is an independent game development studio focused on turning creative ideas into interactive experiences.

We combine programming, game design, storytelling, and technology to create games that encourage exploration, challenge players, and bring imaginative worlds to life.

Our interests span fantasy adventures, puzzle games, interactive environments, and educational experiences. We aim to build projects that are enjoyable to play, thoughtfully engineered, and memorable long after the experience ends.

**Our mission is to create worlds worth exploring and experiences worth remembering.**

## Our Projects

### The Lost Kingdom

The Lost Kingdom is a fantasy game concept centred on exploration, discovery, and adventure.

Players enter a mysterious world shaped by forgotten history, hidden locations, and challenges waiting to be uncovered.

Development focus includes world building, gameplay mechanics, exploration, and storytelling.

Status: In development.

### SQL Detective

SQL Detective is an educational game concept designed to help players develop database skills through detective themed challenges.

Players investigate cases, query databases, analyse information, and solve problems while progressing through increasingly difficult levels.

Development focus includes SQL queries, database fundamentals, interactive challenges, and educational progression.

Status: In development.

### Math Corridor

Math Corridor is an interactive game concept that combines exploration with mathematical problem solving.

Players navigate a game environment, solve challenges, and unlock new paths by applying their mathematical knowledge.

Development focus includes interactive environments, puzzle mechanics, level progression, and gameplay logic.

Status: In development.
